## Acceptance criteria (“Done when”)

### P2 — Form Photo Fixer
- Output meets the rules of 3 real forms (link official notices in `docs/form-notices.md`).
- Computer file details confirm the size in KB.
- Large phone photo works with Wi-Fi off.

### P3 — Resume Builder
- A long resume breaks cleanly across 2 pages.
- The text in the PDF can be selected and copied.
- Loading the saved file restores everything.
- Works with Wi-Fi off.

### P7 — Bill Book
- Totals match hand calculation on 5 bills.
- Works in flight mode, and old bills stay saved.
- A UPI app scans the QR and shows the right amount (no payment needed).
- Installs on a real phone.

### P8 — Auto Rally
- A full race plays on a real phone in flight mode.
- A friend finishes a race with no instructions.
- Crashes and lap counts feel fair.
- The best time survives a restart.

### P13 — Style Spy
- Results checked by hand on 5 websites.
- Saved kits survive a Chrome restart.
- Pasting a kit into a plain page picks up the style.

### P14 — Tab Goblin
- Handles 30 open tabs smoothly.
- A half-filled form is never lost.
- A saved session restores after a Chrome restart.
- Sleeping tabs wake correctly when clicked.

### P16 — Your Own Skill (`/one-page-revision`)
- Starts correctly in a fresh session.
- Works well on 3 different inputs.
- Same task run without the skill shown, with what got better.

### P17 — Same Page, New Design
- Both `before.html` and `after.html` exist.
- The words in both files match.
- The redesign works on a phone.
- 3 design changes named and explained.

## General requirements
- Runs without paid APIs or login; offline where specified.
- Clear, usable UI.
- Prompts saved in order in each project’s `prompts.md`.
- Harmless/sample test data only.
